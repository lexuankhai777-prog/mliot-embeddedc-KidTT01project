   nguyen vong lap standalone cua Member 2.

PHOI HOP VOI DHT11 - MEMBER 1
-----------------------------
- DHT11 DATA -> PA1.
- TIM1 dung cho delay micro-giay.
- USART1: 115200 baud, 8 bits, 1 stop bit, no parity.
- Driver: Core/Src/dht11.c va Core/Inc/dht11.h.

PHOI HOP VOI OUTPUT - MEMBER 3
------------------------------
- USART1: 115200.
- I2C1 dung chung bus voi BMP280.
- SCL -> PB6; SDA -> PB7.
- Green LED -> PB12.
- Yellow LED -> PB13.
- Red LED -> PB14.
- Active-Low Buzzer -> PB15.

Diem quan trong: I2C1 la bus dung chung. BMP280 va LCD khong nen tao hai
bo khoi tao I2C rieng. LCD_Init() phai duoc goi sau khi I2C1 da duoc
khoi tao.

TRINH TU TICH HOP DE XUAT
--------------------------
HAL/System init
 -> GPIO init
 -> TIM1 init
 -> USART1 init
 -> I2C1 init
 -> BMP280 init
 -> UART_Init()
 -> Output_Init()
 -> LCD_Init()
 -> FSM/application loop
      -> doc DHT11
      -> doc BMP280
      -> danh gia trang thai
      -> cap nhat Output/LCD/UART

ERROR HANDLING
--------------
Cac ket qua loi BMP280 da duoc Member 2 kiem thu trong standalone test.
Khi ghep vao FSM, giu nguyen y nghia loi cua driver va map vao co che
error_flags/SystemStatus cua project chung. Neu BMP280 loi, khong dung
du lieu loi de dua vao UPDATE_OUTPUT.
